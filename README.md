**PAGE 1: EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW**

**Purpose**

Give leadership a 30-second snapshot of business health.

KPIs Answer:

How much are we selling?

Are we profitable?

Is growth sustainable?

Are returns under control?

KPIs

Total Sales: $1.56M

Net Sales: $1.47M

Net Profit: $0.16M

Profit Margin: 11.13%

Total Orders: 3001

**Insight:**

Despite healthy revenue, margins are moderate — signaling possible cost or return-related issues.

Sales Trend Over Time (YoY)

2020 consistently outperforms 2019

Strong sales acceleration in Q4

**Insight:**

Growth momentum is strong, especially in festive/peak months.

Sales by Region

West & East are top contributors

South lags significantly

**Insight:**

Regional imbalance suggests expansion or pricing optimization opportunities in South region.

Top 5 Products by Sales

Few products contribute disproportionately to revenue

**Insight:**

Business is product-concentrated → high dependency risk.

Profit Margin Trend

Margin volatility across months

Profit spikes don’t always align with sales spikes

**Insight:**

Higher sales ≠ higher profitability → margin leakage exists.

**PAGE 2: SALES & PROFIT TRENDS**

**Purpose**

Understand growth quality, not just growth volume.

**Insights**

Net Sales YTD vs Last Year

Strong YoY improvement across months

**Insight:**

Sales strategy is working effectively.

Net Profit YTD vs Last Year

Profit growth exists but lags behind sales growth

**Insight:**
Cost pressures or returns are eroding profit gains.

YoY Sales Growth: 161.9%
YoY Profit Growth: 95.16%

**Key Insight:**
⚠️ Sales are growing much faster than profits — unhealthy long-term trend.

Sales by Category

Office Supplies leads sales

Technology & Furniture follow

**Insight:**

Category-level pricing & return behavior need evaluation.

Net Profit by Region

East & West dominate profit

South contributes least

**Insight:**
South region has low profitability — possible high returns or low margins.

**PAGE 3: RETURNS & PROFIT LEAKAGE**

**Purpose**

Identify hidden revenue loss due to product returns.

**Insights**

Returned Sales
$0.1M returned
Return Rate: 5.66%
**Insight:**
 Even small return % causes significant profit loss.

Returned Sales by Region

West has highest returns

South lowest (due to lower sales volume)

 **Insight:**
 High-sales regions also face high return risk.

Return Rate by Category

Furniture highest return rate

Technology close behind

 **Insight:**
 Bulky or fragile products → logistics & quality issues.

Top Products by Return Rate

Several products with 100% return rate

 **Critical Insight**:
These products are pure profit destroyers and need:

Vendor review

Quality checks

Possible delisting

Returned Sales vs Net Profit

Categories with higher returns show lower profit

 **Final Insight:**
 Returns have direct negative impact on profitability.
 
**Outcome:**
Identified mismatch between sales growth and profit growth, highlighted return-driven profit leakage, and recommended focus areas for category & product optimization.
